Remnants of the historic Saladaeng Radiotelegraph Station and the preserved radio mast built in the early 20th century by the “Gesellschaft für Drahtlose Technologie Berlin”. Now called [The Wireless House](https://th.wikipedia.org/wiki/เดอะ_ไวร์เลส_เฮาส์) within the One Bangkok area in Bangkok, Thailand.
